# Idea

## Pitch

The publication the reader is looking at is produced by an automated newsroom. Stories move through a gated pipeline on a public Kanbus board (idea, assignment, research, report, editor select, copywriting, published), drafts are checked against a machine-readable style profile, agents do the reporting and copy, and a human approval is the publish step. That is Papyrus, and it is the same shape as a software factory with a different product coming off the line.

Why now: Papyrus is a product Anthus intends to sell to organizations that need a publication run this way, and the strongest possible proof is the one the reader is standing in. Sequencing matters: write this after the Anth.us editorial migration onto Papyrus (site board) has a few weeks of real board history, and write it from that record, showing actual cards, transitions, and approvals.

Composites are not needed; the board is public and every claim can point at a card.

## Audience

Marketing and communications leads at organizations that publish regularly and cannot staff it. Secondary: anyone evaluating whether agent-produced content can be governed rather than sprayed.
