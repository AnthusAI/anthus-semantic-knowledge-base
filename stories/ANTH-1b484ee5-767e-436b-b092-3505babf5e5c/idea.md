# Idea

## Pitch

The Call Criteria case is the anchor for every credibility claim on the site, and the case page is dated 2023. Refresh it as a multi-year retrospective told by duration and cumulative figures: years in production, models managed, interactions processed, expert review hours, and the accuracy trend the RLHF data flywheel produced over that time. The story is what a human-in-the-loop classification system looks like after years of operation, not after a demo.

Why now: the Plexus classifier lab story needs an at-scale citation with numbers a prospect can check, and the AGENTS.md guidance still says "two years," which undersells the run.

Keep the framing on what was built and what it did. Cumulative and duration-based figures, not point-in-time ones.

## Audience

Prospects evaluating whether Anthus has run this in production rather than in a notebook. Also the engineer who wants to know what the flywheel actually delivered over time.

## Working title

Three years of production RLHF
