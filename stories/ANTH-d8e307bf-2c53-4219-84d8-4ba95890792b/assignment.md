# Assignment

Write the launch article for Chatticus, a named-teammate system designed to run
inside infrastructure the customer controls.

Working title: **Chatticus: The AI Organization You Control**.

## Reader journey

1. Open on the uncomfortable tradeoff in the emerging agent category: Grok Bot
   provides the computer but controls the service; OpenClaw provides ownership
   but makes the user operate the host.
2. Introduce Chatticus as the missing quadrant: productized agent organization,
   customer-controlled boundary.
3. Explain the organization: persistent named roles, shared work, reporting
   lines, review procedures, and human approval gates.
4. Explain model control as operating policy. Different jobs deserve different
   models, settings, and budgets; route ordinary work to the least expensive
   model that clears the quality bar and escalate on evidence.
5. Explain infrastructure and access control: customer AWS or local workers,
   private integrations inside that boundary, and no requirement to turn a
   daily-driver computer into production infrastructure.
6. State what Chatticus does not remove: the human remains responsible for
   direction, access, procedures, and consequential outcomes.
7. Close with the product promise: your agents, your models, your cloud.

## Must

- Connect the product to the arguments in the four recent Anth.us articles.
- Compare Grok Bot, OpenClaw, and Chatticus factually and without caricature.
- Treat model choice as governance and economics, not a cosmetic preference.
- Explain public connectors as optional; private custom integrations may remain
  inside the customer's network boundary.
- Distinguish current implementation from intended product design.
- Use the generated control-room illustration as the hero image.
- Keep the MDX frontmatter `state: unpublished`.

## Must not

- Do not call Chatticus a clone, port, or copy of another product.
- Do not claim that roadmap capabilities are already live.
- Do not present OpenClaw as inherently unsafe or Grok Bot as uniquely bad.
- Do not promise support for model vendors beyond OpenAI today; describe the
  provider interface and routing policy as product direction.
- Do not publish or merge the content to the deployment branch before launch.

## Research

Use the existing Anth.us articles as firsthand context, official OpenClaw and
Grok Bot documentation for competitor claims, and the Chatticus repository for
architecture and current-state claims.
