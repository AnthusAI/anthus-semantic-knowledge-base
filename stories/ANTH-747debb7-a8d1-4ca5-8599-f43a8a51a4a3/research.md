# Research

Primary source: Diogo Almeida, TypeSafe AI, “Introducing System One Models & Jev” (2026-09-15), https://typesafe.ai/blog/introducing-system-one-models-and-jev. The source describes Jev as a structured-output System One Model, trained with RLCD, with parallel sampling and confidence values. It reports 70–500 ms response times, $0.042 per million input tokens, free output, and strong relative workflow-evaluation results; these are vendor claims and the source lists evaluation caveats.

Calibration background: Guo et al., “On Calibration of Modern Neural Networks,” ICML/PMLR 70 (2017), https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/guo17a.html. Use it only to ground the general meaning of calibrated probabilities, not as evidence about Jev.
