# Editor selection

Proceed as a how-to with the ship/no-ship machinery as the centre of gravity, not the accuracy number. The angle: once a human-in-the-loop flywheel has aligned a hosted decision model to your reviewers, the alignment lives in a small calibrated head, and that head is a labeling function you can point at real text; the interesting engineering is deciding where the resulting student may serve.

Why this angle: the two-point win over the teacher is the most quotable number and the least general one, so leading with it would oversell. The per-slice gate, the human-label evaluation and the cascade that did not help are what a reader can reuse on their own data, and the label-source comparison (same model, same 140 labels, direct against distilled) is what ties the piece to the rest of the series.
