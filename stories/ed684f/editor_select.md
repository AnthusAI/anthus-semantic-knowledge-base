# Editor selection

## Selected treatment

Lead with the engineer who has just learned that a protected-cue swap changed a
deployed result. Move immediately to the practical question: who has to explain
that choice? Use Ferguson's remarks as the news peg, then make the argument
through the Meta/Facebook and HireVue sequences.

## Narrative spine

1. A model's decision becomes an organization's decision when someone puts it
   behind a screen, a queue, a shortlist, or an eligibility rule.
2. Ferguson's point: the tool is not an independent actor.
3. Outside evidence can turn into a product and regulatory event; Meta/Facebook
   and HireVue show two different routes.
4. The engineer's response is an operating discipline: record, test, constrain,
   disclose, and revisit.

## Evidence and language guardrails

- Attribute Ferguson's statement to Reuters and call it a chair's remark.
- Attribute the Meta settlement and remedial system to DOJ.
- Attribute the HireVue removal rationale to HireVue; describe EPIC's filing
  separately.
- Treat Biased-Decisions as a way to establish sensitivity, not illegality.
- No legal-advice caveat. No claim that a named advocacy organization has acted
  against the reader's system.
