# Assignment: responsibility after deployment

## Core question

Who is responsible for the decisions after an engineer deploys a decision
model into a regulated workflow?

## Reader

An AI engineer, technical founder, or product leader building scoring,
screening, eligibility, triage, ranking, or routing software.

## Angle

Start from the moment after launch: a civil-rights organization, customer
compliance team, journalist, applicant, or employee produces evidence that
changing a protected cue changes the result. The model cannot take
responsibility. The people who built and deployed the surrounding decision
system have to explain it.

## Research questions

1. What did FTC Chair Andrew Ferguson actually say about agent responsibility?
2. Where have federal agencies treated vendors, screening companies, or
   deployers as answerable for automated decisions?
3. Which cases show advocacy or public evidence producing product, litigation,
   enforcement, or operational pressure?
4. What can Biased-Decisions establish, and what can it not establish?
