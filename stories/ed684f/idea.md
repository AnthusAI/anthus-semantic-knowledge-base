# Who Is Responsible After You Deploy a Decision Model?

## Reader promise

An engineer who places a fast model between a person and a consequential
decision needs to understand that the decision does not belong to the model.
This story will show how an observed protected-cue effect can become a customer,
advocacy, regulatory, or employee-escalation problem after release — and what
records make the system answerable.

## Thesis to test

Responsibility follows the people and organizations that choose the model,
define its task, set its threshold or ranking rule, grant it access, market its
properties, and continue deployment after evidence appears. "The model made the
decision" is not an accountability boundary.

## Evidence bar

- Quote Ferguson accurately: Reuters reports a conference remark, not a new FTC
  rule or decided case.
- Separate a counterfactual benchmark finding from a legal conclusion.
- Prefer agency filings, orders, and company statements to commentary.
