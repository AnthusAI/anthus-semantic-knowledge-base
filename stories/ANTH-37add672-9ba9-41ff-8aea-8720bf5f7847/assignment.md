# Assignment

Write a public Anth.us concept article: from one pair-programming session to administering a virtual company of agents.

Working title: From pair programmer to executive. Ryan may rename.

## Spine

1. Cheap high-value models + long-running high-agency models, now affordable without a corporate sponsor.
2. Most people: one peer session, one agent, one problem. Some parallelize. Some loop. Work still waits on the human push.
3. Scheduled / triggered cloud tasks exist and are rarely a core workflow. Too much hassle.
4. OpenClaw family (Moltbot, ClawdBot, current name): possible, hard, dedicated machine, dangerous energy.
5. Grok Bot as the first practical, widespread version of the operator-as-executive. Middle managers. Product owners. Not the only future.
6. Diagrams: one pair, then three in parallel, then too many direct reports, then hierarchy, then anyone can have that.
7. If you live in that virtual company, you need the quality skills. Link, do not retell.
