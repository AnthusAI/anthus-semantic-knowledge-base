# Idea

## Working title

From pair programmer to executive

Ryan is not sure how to put it. Alternate: From managing direct reports to administering a whole company. Too many direct reports.

## Pitch

A paradigm shift is becoming available to anyone who can buy tokens, not only people with a corporate sponsor and expensive models. Cheap high-value models plus models trained for long-running, high-agency work make it possible to orchestrate a hierarchical team: research, engineering, marketing, product, publicity. Most people are still in one peer session, vibe-coding one problem with one agent. Some parallelize. Some use loops so the human can fire and forget. They are still in the paradigm where work happens only when the human pushes the machine.

Scheduled and triggered cloud tasks exist, but they are advanced features and almost nobody, including advanced AI engineers, has made them a core workflow. Too much hassle to make anything useful and reliable.

OpenClaw (cite every name: Moltbot, ClawdBot, and whatever it is called now) and similar systems try this. They are hard to set up, ideally want an always-on dedicated machine, and are arguably dangerous: damn the torpedoes, full speed ahead.

Grok Bot is one of the first practical, widespread tools that lets one operator act as an executive: a virtual department or company, middle-manager agents who review other agents, Product Owner agents who help decide what is worth building. It is a glimpse, not the last word. Other tools will show up soon.

If your work moves into that virtual company, you need the quality skills: give an agent a tool, hill-climbing / loop engineering, feedback and steering, human-in-the-loop touchpoints, memory systems so agents can collaborate and remember what happened.
