## Cousins (always-on personal-agent runtime, not IDE pair-programmer)

Hermes Agent and nanobot are the clean cousins. Scout is a productization of OpenClaw. Spark is cloud-owned. ZeroClaw says it is not OpenClaw.

- **Hermes Agent** (Nous, Feb 2026) — closest OSS cousin. https://hermes-agent.org/ https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent
- **nanobot** (HKUDS) — “self-hosted personal AI agent runtime”; v0.3.0 2026-07-25. https://github.com/HKUDS/nanobot
- **ZeroClaw** — Rust, own-the-machine; official FAQ says not OpenClaw. https://zeroclaw.net/
- **Gemini Spark** (Google I/O 2026-05-19) — 24/7 but cloud-owned. https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/next-evolution-gemini-app/
- **Microsoft Scout** (2026-06-02) — always-on Autopilot, powered by OpenClaw, not a sibling. https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/blog/2026/06/02/introducing-microsoft-scout-your-always-on-personal-agent/
